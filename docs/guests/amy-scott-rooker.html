At just 14 years old, Amy was sexually abused by a family member. When she told her mother what happened, her response left Amy feeling unsupported and unprotected. The trauma and her mother’s reaction made her feel unsafe in her own home and questioned whether her mother really loved her.
My guest on this episode of About Your Mother is Amy Scott Rooker, author of My Mother is a Dragonfly. Listen in as she vulnerably shares her complicated family story, her healing journey, and her experience with plant medicine.
