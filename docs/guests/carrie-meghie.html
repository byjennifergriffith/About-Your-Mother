Your episode is live

How do you hold onto hope after the worst kind of heartbreak? My guest on this episode of About Your Mother channeled her heartbreak into creating solutions for others.
In 2012, Carrie Meghie experienced a parent’s worst nightmare: after months in the NICU, her 10-month-old son passed away. Listen as she shares how she navigated the loss and continues to live with a broken heart. 
Carrie and her husband used their experience to help others by founding the Jackson Chance Foundation, an organization that covers parking costs for parents visiting their children in Chicago NICUs.
